The Imaging Chain

From photons to digital numbers.

  1. Scene radiance L(λ)

    Light leaving the subject, per unit area, solid angle and wavelength.

  2. Optics — T, MTF

    Transmission scales the signal; the modulation transfer function softens fine detail.

  3. Sensor — QE, full well

    Photons become electrons with quantum efficiency QE, up to the well capacity.

  4. Readout — gain, noise

    Charge is amplified and digitised; read noise sets the floor.

  5. Digital image — DN

    Integers, one per pixel, ready for calibration.

Quantum efficiency

The fraction of incident photons converted to electrons rises through the blue, peaks in the green and falls toward the silicon cut-off near 1100 nm.
