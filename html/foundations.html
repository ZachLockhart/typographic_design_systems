Why Night is thinner

Bright strokes on a dark ground appear wider than the same strokes dark-on-light — irradiation, the effect engravers and type founders compensated for long before screens. Night reduces every engraved weight by about 20 %, switches to greyscale antialiasing, adds a hair of letter-spacing, and pins Didone display faces to a lower optical size so their hairlines do not shimmer away. All ornaments use vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke, so these weights are exact screen pixels at any ornament size.

The Plate.em-sheet

A square plate: --em-heavy outer rule with a hair rule inset 9 px, recalling the plate-mark pressed into paper by an etching press. No rounded corners.

Variants: .em-sheet--single (outer rule only), .em-sheet--plain (no frame).

Grids: .em-grid (0.8 : 1.2 asymmetric), --golden (1 : 1.618), --even, --three. All collapse to one column below 760 px.
