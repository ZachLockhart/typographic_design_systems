Engraved Modernism

A typographic system that reads like a finely engraved scientific instrument, an antique technical encyclopaedia and a modern precision dashboard at once: classical type, guilloché geometry, graduated scales and Swiss discipline in two inks.

Retro-Inkv2.0 exploration — supersedes the Day & Night v1 packages, which remain as reference

The second state

§ 1

Engravers call each revision of a copper plate a state. This is the second state of Retro-Ink: the same visual vocabulary, re-cut with one stylesheet, generated ornament geometry, and a typographic hierarchy that the v1 packages only gestured at. Use the switches above to see every page in Day, Mid, Night or Signal ink, and in each of the three candidate type sets.

Principles

§ 2
  1. Two inks, no tints. Tone is made by line density — hatching, stipple, guilloché — never by grey fills, gradients or shadows.
  2. Ornament carries structure. A medallion frames a numeral, a scale divides a masthead, a ribbon closes a panel. Nothing is applied merely to fill space.
  3. Precision is visible. Graduations, tabular figures, booktabs rules and exact separators show the care of an instrument maker.
  1. Classical voice, modern grid. Literary serif text and Didone display sit on an asymmetric Swiss grid with generous white.
  2. Each ink is tuned. Night is not an inverted Day: line weights, optical sizes and smoothing are adjusted for light-on-dark.
  3. Never fake the data. Example values are illustrative and say so. Figures are drawn, never decorative emoji.

Quick start

§ 4

Link the stylesheet and the ornament script, put em on the body, add em--mid for dimmed Day paper or em--night (plus em--signal) for the dark inks. Ornaments are empty elements with a data-em type; the script draws them in the current ink.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. For offline use, self-host the families of the chosen set and replace the @import in em.css.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="engraved-modernism/em.css">
<script src="engraved-modernism/em-ornaments.js" defer></script>

<body class="em em--night">
  <section class="em-sheet">
    <header class="em-masthead">
      <span class="em-kicker">Field Notes</span>
      <span class="em-meta">Optics · Sensor</span>
    </header>
    <div data-em="scale" data-tail=".14"></div>
    <div class="em-medallion">
      <div data-em="guilloche" data-preset="medallion"></div>
      <div class="em-medallion__face">
        <span class="em-numeral">11</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
</body>
