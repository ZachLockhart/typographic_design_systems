III · Ornaments

Engine-Turned Geometry

Guilloché was cut by a rose engine: a lathe whose cam rocks the work as it turns, so the graver traces phase-shifted waves. These ornaments are computed the same way, in the page's current ink.

Medallions

§ 3.1

Anatomy of the medallion, from the outside in — the layering follows banknote and watch-dial practice:

  1. Heavy rim + hair rule
  2. Tick ring — 180 divisions, every fifth long
  3. Lattice band: 14 waves, 30 lobes, amplitude breathing ×6
  4. Double rule, then a ring of 90 beads
  5. Open face — or, in the seal, a rope band and rosette

Guilloché laboratory

§ 3.2

Band controls apply to band, medallion, seal; rosette controls to rosette and the seal centre. Downloaded SVGs draw in currentColor.

Engraved figures
§ 3.6

  

    

      
Section hatching alternates direction between adjacent elements and grows denser toward each element's leading surface, the way an engraver models curvature with line spacing. Rays are hair lines; the focal point is a solid ink dot; callouts use italic text-face capitals on leader lines.

      
The figure is a schematic, not a ray trace.
